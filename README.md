# 🛡️ SentinelX  

**SentinelX** is a **DeFi Risk & Sentiment Intelligence Layer** built on **BlockDAG**.  
It aggregates **on-chain risk metrics**, **social sentiment signals**, and **community-driven submissions** into one transparent platform—  
helping **investors, DAOs, and protocols** make safer, data-driven decisions in DeFi.  

---

## ❗ Problem  

DeFi has unlocked trillions in value, but investors face critical risks:  

- 🚨 **Protocol Collapses** – hacks, insolvency, rug pulls  
- 💧 **Liquidity Drains** – unstable collateral ratios & sudden losses  
- 📉 **Sentiment Volatility** – hype & fear moving faster than fundamentals  
- 🔍 **Fragmented Tools** – dashboards (DeFiLlama, Dune, etc.) lack trust as single sources of truth  

👉 Each year, **billions are lost** due to poor visibility into risks and unreliable data.  

DeFi investors need a **trusted, real-time, verifiable system** for assessing protocol risks and market sentiment.  

---

## 💡 Solution  

**SentinelX** provides a **real-time intelligence dashboard** that fuses:  

- 📊 **Financial Risk Signals** + 📢 **Social Sentiment Data**  
- Unified into one **Fusion Risk Index (FRI)** score.  

### How it works  
- Tracks key protocols (Aave, Uniswap, MakerDAO, Curve, Lido, etc.) & assets (ETH, BTC, SOL, MATIC).  
- Monitors sentiment across Twitter, Telegram, Reddit, and news feeds.  
- Combines risk + sentiment into a **single transparent score**.  
- Anchors scores on **BlockDAG** → immutable, verifiable, and reusable across DeFi.  

👉 **One reliable source of truth** for DeFi risks, accessible to investors, DAOs, and dApps.  

---

## 🚀 Features  

✅ **Protocol Risk Scores** – token + protocol-specific risk levels  
✅ **Sentiment Engine** – detects FUD, hype, and market mood  
✅ **Community Reports** – submissions from analysts, DAOs & researchers  
✅ **BlockDAG Oracle Layer** – immutable data anchoring  
✅ **Wallet Integration** – check risks before investing
